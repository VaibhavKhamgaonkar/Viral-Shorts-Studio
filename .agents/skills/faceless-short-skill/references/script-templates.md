# High-Retention Explainer Script Templates

These templates are engineered specifically for short-form video explainers (YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok) with high average watch percentages (AWP > 85%).

---

## Template 1: The Counter-Intuitive Mythbuster

**Formula**: [Widely accepted belief] is completely wrong $\rightarrow$ [Startling counter-fact] $\rightarrow$ [How it actually works] $\rightarrow$ [Twist/Loop]

### Example Script: "GPS Doesn't Track Your Phone"
- **Hook (0-3s)**: GPS satellites have no idea where your phone is. In fact, they cannot see you at all.
- **Premise (3-10s)**: Most people think GPS works like a radar tracking device sending your coordinates to government computers.
- **Core Breakdown (10-35s)**:
  - *Beat 1*: Satellites are actually just atomic clocks shouting the exact time into the void 24/7.
  - *Beat 2*: Your phone catches signals from 4 separate satellites, calculates tiny microsecond time delays, and calculates its own distance through math called trilateration.
  - *Beat 3*: Your phone never sends a single byte back up into space. It is a completely silent listener.
- **Loop/Outro (35-45s)**: It does all of this purely by listening to the sky, which explains why... (loops back to Hook: "GPS satellites have no idea where your phone is...")

## Template 2: The "Hidden Machine" (How Infrastructure Works)

**Formula**: [Everyday object] $\rightarrow$ [Massive hidden complexity] $\rightarrow$ [The failure point that prevents catastrophe] $\rightarrow$ [Mind-blowing scale stat] $\rightarrow$ [Loop]

### Example Script: "Undersea Internet Cables"
- **Hook (0-3s)**: If you cut just 10 garden-hose-sized cables right now, the entire global economy halts.
- **Premise (3-10s)**: 99% of all international internet data doesn't travel through space or satellites. It travels across the bottom of the ocean.
- **Core Breakdown (10-38s)**:
  - *Beat 1*: There are over 500 fiber-optic cables resting directly on the ocean floor alongside sharks and shipwrecks.
  - *Beat 2*: Inside each cable are glass strands no thicker than human hair, pulsing lasers carrying terabytes per second across continents.
  - *Beat 3*: Special ships patrol the world's oceans with grappling hooks just to fish them up and splice them when underwater earthquakes strike.
- **Loop/Outro (38-48s)**: Everything you stream, buy, or send relies on these fragile underwater threads, meaning... (loops back to Hook: "If you cut just 10 garden-hose-sized cables right now...")

## Template 3: The "Curious Heuristic" (Science / Psychology)

**Formula**: [Common behavior] $\rightarrow$ [Evolutionary or physiological root cause] $\rightarrow$ [The biological mechanism] $\rightarrow$ [Practical takeaway / Loop]

### Example Script: "Why Time Speeds Up When You Get Older"
- **Hook (0-3s)**: Summer vacations felt like eternity as a kid, but your 20s and 30s vanish in a blink. Why?
- **Premise (3-12s)**: Your brain measures time not in seconds, but in **new memories processed**.
- **Core Breakdown (12-40s)**:
  - *Beat 1*: At age 8, one year represents a massive 12.5% of your entire life, packed with first-time experiences.
  - *Beat 2*: By age 30, a year is barely 3% of your life, mostly governed by automated routines and commute autopilot.
  - *Beat 3*: Because your brain stops recording detailed frames during repeated routines, whole months compress into a single memory file.
- **Loop/Outro (40-50s)**: To slow time back down, you have to break routines and inject novelty, because otherwise... (loops back to Hook: "Summer vacations felt like eternity as a kid...")

## Template 4: The 3-Step Micro-Tutorial

**Formula**: [Desirable outcome in 30 seconds] $\rightarrow$ [Step 1: The foundation] $\rightarrow$ [Step 2: The multiplier] $\rightarrow$ [Step 3: The secret sauce] $\rightarrow$ [Loop]

### Example Script: "3 Simple Rules to Instantly Speak More Confidently"
- **Hook (0-3s)**: Three simple vocal adjustments that make anyone take you seriously within 10 seconds.
- **Step 1 (3-15s)**: Kill the uptone. Stop raising your pitch at the end of sentences like everything is a question. Drop your pitch at the period.
- **Step 2 (15-28s)**: Replace filler words ("um", "like", "you know") with silence. A 2-second pause reads as deliberate authority, not hesitation.
- **Step 3 (28-42s)**: Speak on the exhale. Never inhale mid-sentence; exhale steady air as you deliver your punchline.
- **Loop/Outro (42-50s)**: Master these three subtle habits and you will never need another guide on... (loops back to Hook: "Three simple vocal adjustments that make anyone take you seriously...")

## Key Pacing Benchmarks
- **Average Words**: 75–110 words total.
- **Speech Speed**: 135–150 words per minute.
- **Visual Beats**: 12–16 visual cuts/transitions per 45-second video.
- **Hook Window**: Keep hook text under 14 words so viewers read it in under 2 seconds.
